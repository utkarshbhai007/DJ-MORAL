DJ MORAL - PERSONAL SONGS FOLDER
================================

Please place your 7 custom DJ tracks/songs in this folder.
By default, the music player is configured to look for the following file names:

1. song1.mp3 -> Title: Bollywood Club Mashup
2. song2.mp3 -> Title: Punjabi Dhol Mix
3. song3.mp3 -> Title: EDM Festival Anthem
4. song4.mp3 -> Title: Techno Underground Set
5. song5.mp3 -> Title: Retro Bollywood Fusion
6. song6.mp3 -> Title: Commercial House Grooves
7. song7.mp3 -> Title: Chillout Sunset Session

Note: 
- You can place files with different names, but make sure to update the names and paths in `src/data/songs.ts`!
- The audio player supports .mp3, .wav, .m4a and other web-compatible formats.
